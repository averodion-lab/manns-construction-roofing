Overview

Tree Removal

A dead, leaning, or storm-damaged tree near your home is a risk to your roof, your vehicles, and your family. So is a limb that has already come down on the roof.

We remove trees and get fallen limbs off roofs. Because we’re also roofers, we can inspect for damage and make the repairs once the limb is gone.

What’s included

  • Tree removal
  • Fallen limbs removed from roofs
  • Storm-damaged tree cleanup
  • Limb and debris removal
  • Roof inspection after limb damage
  • Roof repairs after tree damage

A limb fell on my roof. What should I do?

Stay off the roof and keep clear of the area. Call us at 912-246-9486 and we’ll remove the limb, inspect the roof, and protect your home until repairs are made.

Can you repair the roof after the limb is removed?

Yes. We’re roofers, so we can inspect and repair limb damage after it’s removed.

Will insurance cover tree damage?

That depends on your policy and is decided by your insurance company. We can help document damage and guide you through the restoration process.
