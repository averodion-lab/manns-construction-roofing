Can you match my existing siding for a repair?

We’ll do our best to source a close match. With older siding an exact match isn’t always possible — we’ll discuss your options before starting.

Can siding be damaged by storms?

Yes. Hail and wind can crack, dent, or loosen siding. We can inspect and document storm damage.
