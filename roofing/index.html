Overview

Roofing

Your roof is the first line of defense for everything under it. Manns Construction & Roofing provides roofing installation, repairs, replacements, inspections, and maintenance for homes and properties.

How do I know if I need a roof repair or a full replacement?

It depends on the roof’s age, the extent of the damage, and whether problems are isolated or widespread. We’ll inspect the roof and walk you through the options so you can make an informed decision.

Do you work on both shingle and metal roofs?

Yes. We install, repair, and replace both asphalt shingle and metal roofing systems.

How long does a roof replacement take?

Timing depends on the size and pitch of the roof, the material, and the weather. We’ll give you an estimated schedule with your quote.

Are estimates free?

Yes. Call 912-246-9486 or request a quote online and we’ll schedule a time to look at your roof.
