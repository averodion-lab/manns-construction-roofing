Overview

Storm Damage

After a storm, it can be hard to know what’s damaged and what to do first. Wind, hail, and falling limbs can damage roofs, siding, and gutters in ways that aren’t always visible from the ground.

Manns Construction & Roofing inspects the property, documents the damage, explains the recommended repairs, and completes the restoration work.

What should I do right after a storm?

Make sure everyone is safe, take photos of any visible damage from the ground, and avoid climbing on the roof. Then call us to schedule an inspection.

Will my insurance cover the damage?

Coverage depends on your policy and is decided by your insurance company. We can help document damage and guide you through the restoration process.

Can you make temporary repairs?

If there’s active water intrusion, we’ll do what we can to protect your home until permanent repairs can be made.
